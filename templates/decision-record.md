# Decision Record

Add this as a row to `team/decisions/decisions.md`.

## Format

| Date | Decision | Why | Confidence | Check By | Result |
|------|----------|-----|------------|----------|--------|
| {{YYYY-MM-DD}} | {{what you decided — include what you decided NOT to do}} | {{reasoning in 1-2 sentences}} | {{High/Medium/Low}} | {{date to check back}} | — |

## Rules

- Log when you **decide**, not when you ship
- Include what you decided NOT to do
- "Check By" = date to verify whether the decision was right
- Empty "Result" column = accountability gap
- Review every 2 weeks; full monthly review with 5 questions (template in decisions.md)
