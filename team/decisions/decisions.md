# Decision Log

*One line per decision. Reviewed monthly. The artifact a CPO is judged on.*

**How to use:** Add a row when you make a decision — not when you ship something, when you *decide* something. Include what you decided NOT to do. Review the "Result" column every 2 weeks to close the loop.

## Monthly Review Template

*Run on the 1st of each month. Answer these 5 questions:*

1. **Decisions made this month:** How many? What was the biggest one?
2. **Decisions confirmed:** Which ones played out as expected?
3. **Decisions invalidated:** Which ones were wrong? What did you learn?
4. **Decisions still open:** Which ones haven't been checked? Why?
5. **Pattern:** What kind of decisions are you avoiding or delaying?
