# Questions

*The queue of taste and scope questions that would otherwise stop work while someone finds the PM. Skills file a row and continue on their stated assumption. `/judge` clears the queue against `principles.md`; the PM answers only what the judge holds.*

**Last updated:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Owner:** PM (clears `held` rows in the Friday note)

---

## How this file works

- **Anyone or any skill files a row.** One question per row, phrased so a yes/no or a pick is possible. Include the assumption the filer proceeded on, so work did not stop.
- **`/judge` gives every open row one of three outcomes:**
  - `decided`: a principle or a dated decision already answers it. The verdict cites the line (`principles.md S3`, `decisions.md 2026-09-14`).
  - `sent back`: the question is malformed. It asks the human to pick between options the principles already rank, hides a bet inside a ticket, or has no decision in it. The verdict says what to fix; the filer re-files.
  - `held`: nobody has given this judgment yet. The verdict is a one-line summary of what the PM must decide. The Friday note lists held rows; the PM's answer becomes a row in `decisions.md` or `principles.md`, and the row here moves to `decided` citing it.
- **An override is a correction, not an edit.** If the PM disagrees with a `decided` verdict, they add a row to `principles.md` under Judge corrections and change the status here to `overridden`. The judge reads the correction next run.
- Rows older than 30 days in `decided` or `sent back` move to **Closed**.

## Status values

`open` · `decided` · `sent back` · `held` · `overridden` · `closed`
