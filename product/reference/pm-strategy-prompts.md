1. The "Problem Space" Deep Dive

Most PMs jump to solutions. Don't. Use this to force a deep understanding of the user pain point first.

Prompt:
"Act as a Senior PM at a Series B SaaS. I want to build [FEATURE]. Before we discuss solutions, conduct a '5 Whys' analysis to uncover the root user problem. Then, list 3 distinct user personas and their specific pain points related to this problem."


2. The "Pre-Mortem" Analysis

Kill your bad ideas before engineering touches them.

Prompt:
"Imagine we launched [PRODUCT/FEATURE] 6 months ago and it failed miserably. Write a retrospective explaining exactly why it failed, focusing on market fit, adoption barriers, and technical debt. Be brutal."


3. The Competitive Moat Generator

Stop building features everyone else has.

Prompt:
"Analyze [COMPETITOR A] and [COMPETITOR B]. Identify their feature gaps. Now, propose 3 unique value propositions for my product that would be defensible against them for at least 12 months. Focus on 'unsexy' advantages like data lock-in or workflow integration."


4. The "Jobs to be Done" Framework

People hire products to do a job. Find out what that job is.

Prompt:
"Using the Jobs to be Done (JTBD) framework, analyze the motivation behind a user signing up for [YOUR APP]. Break it down into Functional, Emotional, and Social jobs. Create a user story for each."


5. The 1-Pager PRD Draft

Get 80% of the work done in 30 seconds.

Prompt:
"Write a 1-page Product Requirements Document for [FEATURE]. Include: Problem Statement, Success Metrics (North Star + Counter metrics), User Stories, Out of Scope, and a high-level release plan. Keep it concise enough for engineers to read in 5 mins."


6. The Metric Tree decomposition

Don't just say "grow revenue." Break it down.

Prompt:
"Our North Star metric is [METRIC]. Build a KPI tree that breaks this down into leading indicators and input metrics. Show the mathematical relationship between user actions and this top-level goal."


7. The "RICE" Prioritization Helper

Stop arguing with sales about what to build next.

Prompt:
"I have these 5 potential features: [LIST FEATURES]. Score them using the RICE framework (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort). Assume 'Effort' is high for [FEATURE X] and low for [FEATURE Y]. Output a table with the calculated scores and a recommendation."


8. The User Interview Script

Talk to users without asking leading questions.

Prompt:
"I need to interview users about [PROBLEM]. Write a script of 10 open-ended discovery questions. Avoid 'Yes/No' questions. Focus on extracting past behaviors rather than future intent (The Mom Test style)."


9. The "Go-to-Market" Strategy

Building it is only half the battle.

Prompt:
"Outline a GTM strategy for [PRODUCT] targeting [AUDIENCE]. Include a launch timeline, 3 key marketing channels to focus on, and a draft value proposition for the landing page hero section."



10. The Stakeholder alignment email

Manage up without sounding needy.

Prompt:
"Write an update email to the VP of Product regarding the delay of [PROJECT]. The reason is [REASON]. Don't apologize excessively. Focus on the mitigation plan, the new timeline, and what specific support we need from leadership right now."



11. The "Day 1" Retention Strategy

Churn kills SaaS. Fix onboarding first.

Prompt:
"Audit this user onboarding flow: [DESCRIBE FLOW]. Identify 3 friction points where users might drop off. Propose specific psychological triggers or UI changes to increase the 'Aha!' moment speed."


12. The Non-Functional Requirements List

Engineers will love you for this.

Prompt:
"We are building [FEATURE]. List the critical Non-Functional Requirements we must consider, including scalability (users/sec), latency limits, security compliance (GDPR/SOC2), and accessibility standards."



13. The "Press Release" from the Future

Amazon style. Write the announcement first.

Prompt:
"Write a press release dated 1 year from now announcing the massive success of [PRODUCT]. Include quotes from happy customers, specific metrics achieved, and the narrative of how it changed the industry standard."


14. The SQL Query Builder

Stop waiting for data analysts.

Prompt:
"I have a table 'users' with columns [A, B, C] and 'events' with [X, Y, Z]. Write a SQL query to calculate the Week 1 retention rate for users who signed up in the last 30 days."


15. The "Saying No" Template

The most important PM skill.

Prompt:
"A key customer is demanding [FEATURE] that doesn't fit our roadmap. Write a diplomatic but firm email declining the request, explaining why it's not a priority right now, but offering a workaround or explaining how our current roadmap adds value to them in other ways."
